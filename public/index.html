How It Works

  1. 1 Upload the trained XGBoost model and preprocessing artifacts.
  2. 2 Upload the clinical dataset in CSV format.
  3. 3 The system applies the saved training-time encoders.
  4. 4 XGBoost generates stage predictions.
  5. 5 Review results and download the prediction dataset.
